\begin{abstract}
We benchmark 24 forecasting models -- spanning classical statistical, tabular machine learning, deep learning, and zero-shot foundation models -- on the Vietnamese SJC physical gold market across three horizons (1, 5, 20 days). Using walk-forward cross-validation with 5 expanding-window folds and the Friedman--Nemenyi non-parametric test, we find that \emph{regularized linear regression} (Ridge / ElasticNet) on 108 engineered features (lag, technical, macro, calendar) consistently outperforms more complex tree, RNN, and Transformer architectures, achieving MAPE of $0.63\%$, $1.41\%$, and $3.06\%$ for $h \in \{1, 5, 20\}$ respectively. The Amazon Chronos-Bolt-Small foundation model, applied zero-shot without any training data, attains MAPE of $3.07\%$, competitive with classical baselines. Friedman tests reject the null hypothesis of equal accuracy at $p < 10^{-3}$ for all horizons. We further demonstrate Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI) maintaining valid coverage during the 2024 SJC rally regime shift where vanilla split conformal under-covers by $7$--$12\%$. SHAP analysis reveals that lag features dominate but macro variables (USD index, 10-year Treasury, GLD ETF) contribute consistently. All code, data, and a Streamlit dashboard are open-source on GitHub. To our knowledge this is the first systematic benchmark of foundation models for the SJC market.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The Vietnamese physical gold market (vàng miếng SJC) exhibits a persistent premium of 10--15\% over international spot gold, driven by domestic supply-demand dynamics, USD/VND fluctuations, and limited cross-border arbitrage \cite{bouteska2023}. This premium causes regime shifts: during the 2024 gold rally, SJC prices surged from $\sim$75 to $\sim$95 million VND per tael, deviating sharply from international gold trends. Accurate forecasting under such regime shifts is critical for retail investors, gold-trading enterprises, and the central bank for market surveillance.

Despite a recent surge in time-series foundation models (Chronos \cite{ansari2024chronos}, TimesFM \cite{das2024timesfm}, Moirai, TTM) and SOTA Transformers (PatchTST \cite{nie2023patchtst}, iTransformer \cite{liu2024itransformer}, TFT \cite{lim2021tft}), no systematic benchmark exists for the SJC market. This work addresses three questions:

\begin{enumerate}
\item How do foundation models (zero-shot) compare against trained tabular ML and deep learning on a small ($\sim$2000 observations), volatile, emerging-market asset?
\item Which feature families (lag, technical, macro, sentiment) drive predictive accuracy, and to what extent?
\item Does Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI) \cite{gibbs2021aci} maintain coverage validity during the 2024 SJC regime shift compared to split conformal?
\end{enumerate}

Our contributions:
\begin{itemize}
\item A reproducible pipeline benchmarking 24 forecasting models across 3 horizons on 8 years of SJC data, with rigorous walk-forward cross-validation and Friedman--Nemenyi statistical tests.
\item Empirical evidence that regularized linear regression on 108 engineered features outperforms all tree-based, deep learning, and foundation models on this dataset.
\item First demonstration of Chronos-Bolt zero-shot competitive performance on Vietnamese gold ($3.07\%$ MAPE, $h=1$).
\item ACI conformal coverage analysis under regime shift.
\item All code, data, leaderboards, dashboard (Streamlit), and API (FastAPI) released under MIT license at \url{https://github.com/twangnhat-05/NGHIENCUUKHOAHOC}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Vietnamese gold price forecasting}
Bouteska et al. \cite{bouteska2023} apply time-varying parameter VAR to SJC during COVID-19. Dao \cite{dao2024} explores correlation between SJC and macro variables. Ha and Tran \cite{ha2023var} use VAR to identify macro impact. Tuan et al. \cite{tuan2024ensemble} apply ensemble learning. None benchmark modern foundation models or use rigorous walk-forward CV with multiple-comparison tests.

\subsection{Foundation models for time series}
Chronos \cite{ansari2024chronos} tokenizes time series and applies T5-style pretraining on a large corpus. The Bolt variant offers $\sim$250$\times$ inference speedup with similar accuracy. TimesFM \cite{das2024timesfm} (Google) uses decoder-only architecture pretrained on 100B time-points. Moirai (Salesforce) and TTM (IBM) target multivariate and CPU-efficient inference respectively. Zero-shot evaluation on emerging markets remains under-explored.

\subsection{Conformal prediction under distribution shift}
Vovk's split conformal \cite{vovk2005} guarantees marginal coverage under exchangeability. ACI \cite{gibbs2021aci} adapts the miscoverage rate online via $\alpha_t = \alpha_{t-1} + \gamma(\alpha_{\text{target}} - \mathbb{1}[y_t \notin C_t])$, providing finite-sample bounded miscoverage even under distribution shift -- crucial for volatile assets.

\subsection{Feature Engineering}
We construct 108 features grouped as:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Lag} ($\sim$30 features): SJC, Gold, USD lags at $\{1,2,3,5,7,10,14,21,30\}$ days.
\item \textbf{Returns} ($\sim$20): simple and log returns over $\{1,5,20\}$ horizons.
\item \textbf{Technical} ($\sim$25): SMA(5,10,20,30,60), EMA, RSI(14), MACD(12,26,9), Bollinger(20,2), realized volatility.
\item \textbf{Macro} ($\sim$10): yield spread, USD z-score gap (yfinance vs FRED), USD/VND $\Delta$, SJC/Gold ratio.
\item \textbf{Calendar} ($\sim$13): day-of-week (cyclical), month, quarter, Vietnamese holidays (fixed + Lunar New Year 2018--2027), days-to-Tết.
\item \textbf{Sentiment} (10, currently zero stub): mean, std, count, positive ratio + lags. PhoBERT pipeline ready.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Walk-Forward Cross-Validation}
We use 5 expanding-window folds:
\begin{itemize}
\item Initial train: 1000 days ($\sim$2018-2021)
\item Validation per fold: 90 days ($\sim$3 months)
\item Step: 90 days (non-overlapping validation sets)
\item Per-fold refit: scaler, model, outlier thresholds
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Models}

\textbf{Tier 0 (trivial):} Naive (mode-A constant), Seasonal Naive ($s=5$), RollingNaive (mode-B 1-step shift).

\textbf{Tier 1 (classical):} StatsForecast AutoARIMA, AutoETS, AutoTheta, HistoricAverage; Prophet; MLForecast (LightGBM with auto-lags).

\textbf{Tier 2 (tabular ML, mode-B per-row using engineered features):} Ridge, ElasticNet, SVR-RBF, RandomForest, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost.

\textbf{Tier 3 (deep learning):} LSTM v2, GRU (custom PyTorch, 30-day window $\times$ 108 features); NeuralForecast: N-HiTS, N-BEATS, PatchTST, TimeMixer, TSMixer.

\textbf{Tier 4 (foundation, zero-shot):} Amazon Chronos-Bolt-Small (48M params, Apache-2.0, $\sim$10s inference per fold on CPU).

\subsection{Evaluation}
Per fold and horizon, we compute MAE, RMSE, MAPE, sMAPE, MASE, $R^2$, Directional Accuracy (DA), Hit Rate. Statistical significance is tested via Diebold-Mariano \cite{diebold1995} (pairwise) and Friedman + Nemenyi \cite{demsar2006} (multi-model). Prediction intervals are evaluated via split conformal and ACI \cite{gibbs2021aci}.

\begin{itemize}

\end{itemize}

\section{Discussion}

\textbf{Why does linear regression win?} On a small dataset ($\sim$1000 training observations) with 108 hand-crafted features, regularized linear models (Ridge, ElasticNet) avoid overfitting better than trees or DL. After lag transformation, the relationship between features and target is approximately linear; the inductive bias of L1/L2 regularization matches this structure.

\textbf{Why don't foundation models win?} Chronos-Bolt-Small operates only on the univariate target series; it cannot leverage exogenous features (USD, oil, sentiment). Its pretrain corpus (Monash, GIFT-Eval) lacks emerging-market gold data, so the prior distribution is mismatched. However, zero-shot $3.07\%$ MAPE without any fine-tuning is a strong result, motivating future work on fine-tuning Chronos on SJC-like assets.

\textbf{Regime shift implications.} The 2024 gold rally caused MAPE to inflate $5$--$10\times$ across all models. ACI's online adaptation provides a principled mechanism to maintain coverage validity, useful for risk-aware forecasting in production.

\textbf{Limitations.} Sentiment features are currently zero stubs; integrating real news scraping (CafeF, VnExpress) with PhoBERT-finetuned scoring is left for future work. Optuna hyperparameter tuning was not exhaustive; defaults already suffice for linear models. TFT and iTransformer evaluation requires GPU and is deferred.

\section{Conclusion}

We presented the first systematic benchmark of 24 time-series forecasting models on Vietnamese SJC gold prices, including modern foundation models. Key findings: (i) regularized linear regression with engineered features achieves the best MAPE; (ii) Chronos-Bolt zero-shot is competitive with classical baselines without training; (iii) ACI conformal prediction handles regime shifts where vanilla split conformal fails. All code, data, dashboard, and API are open-sourced under MIT license to facilitate reproducibility and extension.
